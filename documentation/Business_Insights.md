# Business Insights

## 1. Overall Performance

- Total orders: approximately 2K
- Total revenue: approximately 962.40K
- Total units sold: approximately 10K
- Average shipping delay: approximately 0.62 days

The dashboard provides an overall view of sales performance and shipping efficiency.

## 2. Department Performance

Fan Shop is the highest-revenue department among the top-performing departments, followed by Apparel.

Golf, Footwear, and Outdoors contribute comparatively lower revenue.

This indicates that Fan Shop is an important contributor to overall department-level revenue.

## 3. Geographic Performance

Maharashtra is the highest-revenue state among the top five states, followed by Uttar Pradesh.

Karnataka and Tamil Nadu show similar revenue levels, while Rajasthan contributes the lowest revenue among the top five.

This highlights geographical differences in sales contribution.

## 4. Customer Segment Analysis

The Consumer segment contributes the highest revenue, followed by Corporate and Home Office.

This indicates that Consumer customers are the primary revenue contributor in the dataset.

## 5. Product Performance

The highest-revenue product generates approximately 160K in revenue, significantly higher than several other top-performing products.

The analysis helps identify products that contribute disproportionately to overall revenue.

## 6. Shipping Performance

Shipping performance varies across shipping modes.

Second Class has the highest average shipping delay, while Standard Class has the lowest late-delivery percentage among the shipping modes shown in the dashboard.

First Class shows a high late-delivery percentage despite having a relatively lower average delay.

This demonstrates why both average delay and late-delivery percentage should be considered when evaluating shipping performance.

## 7. Revenue vs Units Sold

The Revenue vs Units Sold analysis indicates that higher unit sales do not always result in proportionally higher revenue.

Some products generate relatively high revenue with fewer units sold, suggesting that product value or selling price also influences revenue.

## 8. Overall Conclusion

The analysis identifies the major revenue-contributing departments, states, customer segments, and products while also highlighting differences in shipping performance.

The dashboard can be used to monitor sales performance, identify high-performing business areas, and understand potential shipping-related issues.
